How to check soil moisture
  1. Push a finger 2–3 cm into the soil, about up to the first knuckle.
  2. Dry and crumbly soil usually needs water. Cool, slightly damp soil can wait. Sticky or dripping soil should not be watered.
  3. Lift the pot. A dry pot feels much lighter than a freshly watered one.
  4. Push a wooden stick to the bottom and pull it out. If dark, wet soil sticks to it, wait.
